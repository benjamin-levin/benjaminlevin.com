## Detail

At **BeHuman**, I am a Product Owner responsible for the patient interface and patient-chart analysis optimization. I started in June 2026. My role connects the experience a patient sees with the engineering and evaluation work needed to make it dependable.

### Patient experience and product ownership

I own priorities and requirements for the patient-facing interface, with an emphasis on an approachable experience that explains information clearly and supports people with different levels of familiarity with healthcare and technology.

The work includes shaping patient-portal journeys, clarifying acceptance criteria, coordinating implementation with engineering, and reviewing how changes fit together as a usable experience. Product ownership means making the tradeoffs explicit and carrying them through delivery.

### Patient-chart analysis and evaluation

I work on improving how chart analysis becomes useful product output: what information is relevant, how results should be presented, and how to evaluate whether an AI-assisted feature is consistent with its supporting evidence.

My responsibilities include analysis-quality and efficiency priorities, evaluation workflows, and discussion of latency and cost tradeoffs with the engineering team. I have also worked on reusable QA approaches for AI-driven features, including checks for unsupported claims and overly agreeable answers.

### Cross-functional delivery

I translate patient and clinical needs into engineering work, help resolve scope and priority questions, and coordinate review and release readiness. The public description stays at the level of responsibilities and contribution areas; patient information and internal product details are not part of this portfolio.
